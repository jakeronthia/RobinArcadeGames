FAQ

Everything you need to know about ROBIN Arcade and $ROBIN.

What is ROBIN Arcade?

A collection of free arcade games you play right in your browser. Every game stars the same squad of NEAR memes, and the whole arcade is powered by $ROBIN. New games drop here first.

Do I need a wallet or to pay anything?

No: every game is free and works without a wallet. You need a NEAR account only to play for the pot (a free one is created for you in one click) or for the $ROBIN holder badge on your profile.

How does the prize pool work?

Every game can be played for free or for the pot. A game for the pot costs about $0.10 in $ROBIN and gives you a 1 in 2,000 chance to win the instant jackpot on the spot, in any game. Players with a free account also get up to 10 free jackpot rolls a day, at 1 in 200,000. Each game for the pot splits automatically: 50% to the weekly prize pool, 30% to the jackpot, 15% burned forever, 5% to next week. Every week 60% of the pool goes to the Vault Cup top 10 and 40% to the 10 most active players. A contract on NEAR runs every rule and has no owner. It runs on testnet with free demo tokens for now: see Prize Pool.

Can anyone change the results?

Every game you play for the pot is a transaction on NEAR. Payments, the split of every play, jackpot draws and payouts are recorded on-chain and can’t be changed by anyone. The prize pool contract has no owner keys, so its rules can’t be changed either. Vault Cup scores are checked by the contract itself, which replays every move. The games themselves run in your browser: for Rush, Brawl, Patrol, Night Shift and Ventures the payment and any jackpot win are on-chain, while the Vault Cup is verified move by move.

How do I get $ROBIN?

You can swap for it on Ref Finance and follow the price on DEX Screener. Always double check the contract address before you swap.

Where are my scores saved?

In your browser, on this device. If you clear your browser data or switch device, your records start again.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Patrol, Rush and Brawl play best with the phone sideways. Night Shift and ROBIN Ventures are made for the phone held upright.

Can I install ROBIN Arcade as an app?

Yes. On iPhone open the site in Safari, tap Share and then "Add to Home Screen". On Android open it in Chrome, tap the menu and then "Install app". It opens full screen like a normal app.

How do I get into the Hall of Fame?

Weekly champions are announced on X and added to the Hall of Fame on the homepage.

Is any of this financial advice?

No. ROBIN Arcade is a set of games made for fun. Nothing on this site is financial advice. Crypto is risky, so only use what you can afford to lose.
