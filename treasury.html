The rules, as the contract runs them
  1. Tickets burn. A daily ticket costs 10 tokens: half is sent to an address nobody controls, 40% fills today's pot, 10% the weekly reserve.
  2. Moves, not scores. You send the list of moves you made. The contract replays the whole defense with the same rules as the game and decides if you beat today's target.
  3. Midnight ritual. Once the day is over anyone can close it. Ticket holders who beat the target split the pot. If nobody did, the thieves take it: the pot is burned.
  4. The city plays together. Every verified defense counts toward the weekly goal. Goal met: the reserve goes to every player of the week. Missed: it is burned.
  5. Holders steer the game. Locked votes pick the hero of the week, who is stronger in everyone's next defense.
  6. Records that stay. Badges are written to your player record and cannot be sold or moved. The player with the most wins in a week becomes Vault Keeper.
