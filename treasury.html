      The rules, as the contract runs them
      1. Free or for the pot. Every game can be played for free. A game for the pot costs about $0.10 in $ROBIN (1 tROBIN on testnet).
      2. Instant jackpot. Each game for the pot has a 1 in 2,000 chance to win the jackpot on the spot. Free players with an account get a few free rolls a day at 1 in 200,000.
      3. Burned on every game. 15% of each game for the pot goes to an address nobody controls. The more people play, the less $ROBIN there is.
      4. The weekly pool. Half of every game for the pot fills the weekly pool. 60% goes to the Vault Cup top 10, 40% to the 10 most active players. Ranks get 25, 18, 13, 10, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4 and 4% of each part.
      5. Moves, not scores. Vault Cup players send their moves. The contract replays the whole defense with the game's own rules, so a score cannot be invented.
      6. No owner. Anyone can close a finished week, and the contract has no withdraw method and no keys.
